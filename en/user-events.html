Latest events

Open Latest events from the main window. This is the live feed of everything StreamKit+ recorded: donations, follows, subscriptions, raids, channel points, chat-driven custom events, manual timer edits, cooperative sync, and more.

Newest records stay at the top. Scroll down to load older ones. A Back to top button returns you to the newest events.

What a row shows

  • Platform icon and event-type icon
  • Username, amount (in your display currency), and message when the event has them
  • Timestamp
  • Optional action chips for effects that ran with this event

http(s) links in messages are clickable when that option is on in Chat window settings. The first time you open a new domain, the app asks for confirmation.

Action chips

Depending on what fired for that event, you may see:

Chip Action
Overlay Play the effect again, or skip the one currently playing
Sound Replay the sound
Timer Shows how much time was added or subtracted
Hotkey Shows which preset ran
Cooperative sync Shows a synced effect from a room
Addon Play or stop media attached by an addon

Use these to recover a missed overlay or to test that a trigger is wired correctly.

Delete a record

Right-click a row and choose delete. That removes the local dashboard record only — it does not refund a donation or unsay a chat message.

Empty feed

If nothing appears, a streaming or donation addon is missing, disabled, or not signed in. Check the status bar on the main window, then the addon’s settings.
